**ProtoMed-LLM** is an end-to-end automatic evaluation pipeline for Large Language Models on scientific protocol generation tasks, incorporating LLM-as-a-judge techniques.

## Key Contributions

- Developed end-to-end **automatic evaluation pipeline** for LLMs on scientific protocol generation
- Designed the benchmark dataset **BIOPROT 2.0**, curating biomedical protocols and generating pseudocode for synthesis and benchmarking
- Incorporates LLM-as-a-judge techniques for evaluation

## Publication

Presented at **CHITA 2024** (Conference on Health IT and Analytics)

Work also presented at LREC-COLING 2024 and submitted to EMNLP 2024.
